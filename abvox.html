The Signal Path

Six iconic studio processors in one coherent chain.

Instead of juggling half a dozen mismatched plugins, ABVox provides a calibrated vocal rack where every processor interacts naturally with the next.

Module 01

Tone Stage

Class-A 1073 Warmth & Tone

Inspired by legendary British Class-A console channels. Features discrete transformer saturation, warm low-mid contour at 218 Hz (+1.3 to +3.85 dB), a clarity notch at 2.1 kHz, presence bite at 7.9 kHz, and a shimmering 16 kHz air shelf.

Module 02

Dynamics

1176 & LA-2A Chain Compressor

The holy grail of vocal dynamics: a dual-stage compressor combining ultra-fast FET peak control with smooth, musical optical leveling. Tames runaway transients while keeping the body of the vocal firmly pinned to the front of the mix.

Module 03

De-Esser

Algorithmic Dual-Path Architecture

Advanced algorithmic de-essing using dual-path dynamic frequency splitting (voiced vs. sibilant). Equipped with 32-sample sliding-window RMS energy detection and dynamic resonance auto-notch filtering (6.5 kHz) to eliminate harsh 'S' and 'T' sounds without lisping.

Module 04

Delay Engine

Ping-Pong Tape Echo & S1 Stereo

Authentic analog tape delay with 0.1 Hz wow & flutter modulation. Features Abbey Road send filtering (188 Hz low-cut, 3.5 kHz high-cut), progressive 1.77 kHz feedback darkening with tape saturation, and a cross-fed ping-pong bounce with a stereo width shuffler.

Module 05

Doubler

Reel ADT Type Doubler

Modelled after Abbey Road's legendary Artificial Double Tracking (ADT) reel-to-reel tape technique. Adds stereo micro-pitch detuning, dual modulated voice delay lines, and wide stereo spread to make lead vocals sound deep, rich, and dimensional.

Module 06

Space & Reverb

Chamber Reverb Plates Style

A blend of acoustic chamber diffusion and vintage plate reverb. Tuned with a 55.6 ms pre-delay, all-pass comb filtering network, and Abbey Road post-reverb filtering (328 Hz HPF, 2411 Hz LPF, and 2.7 kHz smooth shelf) so the reverb never clouds the dry vocal.
